Sovereignty

Conversation agents.

To absorb the repetitive work of replying to customers and staff, in several languages, without your data ever leaving Switzerland.

Without an agent

The team spends 8–12 hrs/week sorting and replying to multilingual reviews. Tone varies between staff members. Replies take 24–72 hrs to go out.

With the Arkneys agent

The agent drafts 90% of replies in the right tone and the right language within 15 minutes. The manager validates in 2 minutes. Perfect consistency, turnaround divided by 10.

Field example

In the 4–5★ hotels we support in French-speaking Switzerland, the front-desk team spends on average 1 hr 45 min a day sorting and replying to reviews. An agent calibrated on the Brand Bible absorbs 70% of that volume with human validation, freeing the team for in-person hospitality and service.

Eight use cases,
concrete.

  • Sorting and replying to Booking, Google and TripAdvisor reviews in 4 languages
  • 24/7 concierge on your website (multilingual chat, human escalation)
  • Internal FAQ for staff (procedures, HR, suppliers)
  • First-level support with routing to the right person
  • Replies to simple quote requests with information gathering
  • Pre-qualification of inbound leads before handover to sales
  • Personalised appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Post-service satisfaction surveys

The Arkneys method
for this agent.

Calibration on your Brand Bible (tone, forbidden words, house phrasing). Systematic human validation during the first 4 weeks, then an adjusted rate. Dashboard: autonomous resolution rate, hours saved per week, customer satisfaction.
